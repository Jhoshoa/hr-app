## Common HR SaaS Features / Modules

### 1. Employee Database (HRIS Core)
- Central employee records
- Org charts
- Document storage
- Role-based access / permissions
- Custom fields

### 2. Payroll Management
- Salary calculation & deductions
- Tax filings (W-2, T4, etc.)
- Direct deposit
- Pay stubs & year-end reports
- Multi-country payroll (for global platforms)

### 3. Time & Attendance
- Clock in/out (web + mobile)
- Biometric / geo-fencing
- Overtime calculation
- Shift scheduling
- Timesheet approvals

### 4. Leave & Absence Management
- PTO / vacation requests & approvals
- Sick leave, parental leave, jury duty
- Accrual policies
- Calendar integration (Outlook, Google)
- Balance tracking

### 5. Recruitment / Applicant Tracking (ATS)
- Job posting to boards (LinkedIn, Indeed, etc.)
- Resume parsing
- Interview scheduling & scorecards
- Candidate pipeline management
- Offer letter generation

### 6. Onboarding & Offboarding
- New-hire checklists
- Digital document signing (I-9, W-4)
- Role-based task automation
- Offboarding checklists
- Exit interviews

### 7. Performance Management
- OKR / goal setting & tracking
- Performance reviews (annual, quarterly)
- 360-degree feedback
- Continuous feedback / check-ins
- Competency frameworks

### 8. Learning & Development (LMS)
- Course catalog & assignment
- Employee training tracking
- Certifications & compliance training
- Content authoring
- Skill gap analysis

### 9. Benefits Administration
- Health, dental, vision enrollment
- Open enrollment management
- Plan comparison tools
- COBRA / FSA / HSA
- Pension / 401(k) management

### 10. Compensation Management
- Salary bands & benchmarking
- Bonus & commission tracking
- Equity management
- Compensation review cycles
- Budget planning

### 11. Employee Engagement & Culture
- Pulse surveys / eNPS
- Recognition & rewards
- Company announcements / social feed
- 1:1 meeting templates
- Anonymous feedback

### 12. HR Analytics & Reporting
- Pre-built dashboards
- Headcount & turnover reports
- Diversity metrics
- Custom report builder
- Predictive analytics (AI-driven)

### 13. Employee Self-Service (ESS)
- Profile & contact updates
- Pay slip access
- Leave requests
- Benefits selection
- Training enrollment

### 14. Manager Self-Service (MSS)
- Team org chart & directory
- Approval workflows (leave, expense)
- Performance review initiation
- Time-off calendar view
- Team analytics

### 15. Compliance & Security
- GDPR, CCPA, SOC 2, ISO 27001
- Audit trails
- Data retention policies
- Role-based permissions
- Multi-factor authentication

### 16. Integrations / API
- Payroll providers
- Accounting (QuickBooks, Xero)
- Productivity (Slack, Teams, Google Workspace)
- Background checks (Checkr)
- SSO (Okta, OneLogin)

### 17. Mobile App
- iOS + Android
- Time tracking & leave on the go
- Push notifications
- Approval actions
- Employee directory
